David Ritter

Ground control & networking

  1. Phase 3 · Implementation

    Two boards, in 3D

    Modeled both of our radio boards in 3D: the ESP32 board in the handheld remote and the Pico board in the gateway.

  2. Phase 3 · Implementation

    Cases, designed and printed

    Designed a case for each board in Fusion 360, then 3D-printed them.

  3. Oct 23, 2026 · Demo B

    Firmware and the radio bridge

    Took on the ESP32 firmware and the link between the Pico board and the drone’s Heltec radio, two radios that frame LoRa packets differently.

Also worked on

  • Pixhawk setup and tuning, with Grant
  • UART integration, with Grant
  • Ground control interface
  • Video streaming, with Sergio
  • Integration testing, with Grant and Leonel

Grant Navarro

Flight dynamics & avionics

  1. Phase 3 · Implementation

    Where everything sits

    Sketched how the GPS, the Pixhawk, the downward lidar and the laser rangefinder on its servo sit relative to each other, so a distance measured from the drone can become a position on the ground.

  2. Phase 3 · Implementation

    The math, then the test

    Worked out the geometry from the drone’s height and the rangefinder’s reading to the target’s position, then tested the laser rangefinder and the equation that sets the servo’s angle.

  3. Phase 3 · Implementation

    A servo stands in for the gimbal

    Tested a servo standing in for the gimbal’s own, with potentiometers simulating the values it will read from the Pixhawk.

  4. Oct 23, 2026 · Demo B

    Onto the real gimbal

    Testing the servos on the gimbal itself, and bringing the lidar’s data into the Pixhawk and the ground station. Next: the test code onto the Jetson, and the infrared camera.

Also worked on

  • Power system and board fabrication, with David and Vincent
  • Pixhawk setup and tuning, with David
  • UART integration, with David
  • System integration, with Leonel and Vincent
  • Sensor and power testing, with Vincent
  • Integration testing, with David and Leonel
  • Flight testing, with Leonel

Vincent Munoz

Electromechanical & power systems

  1. Phase 3 · Implementation

    The power distribution board

    Designed the board that carries the 4S battery to the four ESCs and makes the 12 V, 5 V and 3.3 V rails for the Jetson, the servos and the sensors.

  2. Oct 23, 2026 · Demo B

    The board arrives

    The fabricated board had just arrived, and soldering was under way. The smallest surface-mount parts were next, with David, a hot plate and a digital microscope.

Also worked on

  • Power system and board fabrication, with David and Grant
  • System integration, with Grant and Leonel
  • Sensor and power testing, with Grant

Leonel Cordova

Middleware & system integration

  1. Phase 3 · Implementation

    A home for the Jetson

    Designed and installed the Jetson Orin Nano’s enclosure and its mount on the airframe.

  2. Phase 3 · Implementation

    A two-axis camera gimbal

    Designed, printed and assembled the servo gimbal that carries the camera.

  3. Phase 3 · Implementation

    Live telemetry in the GUI

    Connected the Pixhawk 6X to the VENATOR GUI over a SiK telemetry radio. The port is found on its own; roll, pitch, heading, battery and flight mode update 10 times a second, and the downward lidar’s distance to the floor 5 times a second.

  4. Oct 23, 2026 · Demo B

    Checked on the bench

    Verified the wireless MAVLink connection and live telemetry through the SiK radio, and the lidar’s readings feeding PX4’s height estimate. Next: outdoor range tests, and the camera’s view in the GUI.

Also worked on

  • Jetson setup, ROS 2 and AI, with Sergio
  • System integration, with Grant and Vincent
  • Integration testing, with David and Grant
  • Flight testing, with Grant
  • AI detection testing, with Sergio

Sergio Arreola

Perception & edge AI

  1. Oct 9, 2026

    Finding people and animals

    Switched the onboard detector to MegaDetector v6, which finds people and animals, and gave it a memory: each detection keeps a track ID, a confidence and a distance.

  2. Oct 21, 2026

    Motors spin over our own radio

    Wrote the handheld remote’s menu (test each motor, test all four, hover, status) and spun the motors on the real aircraft from it, over the 915 MHz link. The same day, the Pico gateway learned to talk to the drone.

  3. Oct 23, 2026 · Demo B

    The numbers at Demo B

    The LoRa link delivered 20 of 20 packets with no loss, at about 435 ms round trip. The OAK-D Pro drew about 3.1 W, and the whole system ran inside the Jetson’s 7 W power mode, light enough for battery flight.

  4. Nov 6, 2026

    Find the worker in the yellow vest

    Taught the camera to lock onto a yellow construction vest and ignore everyone else, with search controls and calibration on the operator’s page and an alert that sounds on iPhones too.

  5. Nov 19–20, 2026

    A tougher link, a 4 ft hover

    Hardened the LoRa command link end to end, added an indoor hover mode, and raised the remote’s hover demo to 4 ft (1.22 m).

Also worked on

  • Buying the parts
  • Jetson setup, ROS 2 and AI, with Leonel
  • Video streaming, with David
  • AI detection testing, with Leonel
